JP EXPRESS PACKAGING CHECKLIST

[ ] Choose a strong box with enough room for cushioning
[ ] Wrap fragile items individually
[ ] Fill empty space so contents cannot move
[ ] Use water-resistant inner protection where useful
[ ] Seal all openings with quality packing tape
[ ] Remove old labels and barcodes
[ ] Add a clear address label and phone number
[ ] Mark fragile or orientation requirements clearly
[ ] Weigh and measure the final packed shipment

For unusual, valuable or temperature-sensitive items, contact JP Express before packing.
