UNDERSTAND YOUR LOGISTICS NEEDS

Built around the way online sellers ship

Online sellers often need a predictable way to send customer orders abroad, ship product samples to buyers or reviewers, and handle repeat dispatches as order volume grows. Shipment size, destination coverage, packaging, tracking expectations and order frequency help determine which service may fit each type of shipment.

COMMON CHALLENGES

Plan for the details that matter

Shipment requirements vary. These are the considerations e-commerce and online sellers most often ask about.

01

Small-parcel volume and cost control

Individually-priced parcels can become expensive quickly. Consolidation, packaging efficiency and service choice materially affect per-order economics.

02

Customer-facing delivery expectations

Customers expect clear delivery estimates and reliable tracking. Realistic promises — matched to the actual service chosen — matter more than optimistic marketing.

03

Destination restrictions on consumer goods

Batteries, liquids, cosmetics, food, and some consumer electronics face destination-specific rules. Order acceptance requires checking before dispatch, not after.

04

Recurring shipment coordination

Growing sellers quickly move from a few parcels a week to regular volume. A repeatable process for pickup, documentation and reporting saves significant operational time.

DOCUMENTATION & CUSTOMS

Check requirements before dispatch

Depending on the product, transaction and destination, documents may include a commercial invoice, packing list, accurate product description, HS code, certificate of origin or regulatory paperwork. This is a list of possible documents, not a universal checklist.

Product classification, import restrictions, duties and regulatory requirements can vary by destination. Confirm current requirements for your shipment before booking, and check before accepting an order for international dispatch.

PACKAGING & PREPARATION

Prepare your shipment

  • Choose mailers or cartons appropriate to product weight and fragility.
  • Prevent internal movement and protect delicate or liquid-sensitive contents where accepted.
  • Use accurate recipient details and product descriptions on every parcel.
  • Confirm destination restrictions before accepting an order for international dispatch.
  • Keep the packed parcel under the weight and size limits of your chosen service.

RESTRICTED ITEMS & COMPLIANCE

Declare contents accurately

Possible considerations for online sellers

Batteries, liquids, cosmetics, food, aerosols and some consumer electronics carry destination-specific or carrier-specific restrictions. Adult items, counterfeits and prohibited goods are refused. A precise product description with accurate HS code reduces the risk of delays, refusals or returned parcels — always confirm rules for your destination and product before accepting an order.

COST & TRANSIT

Understand what affects your shipment

Shipping cost factors

Cost may depend on actual or volumetric weight, parcel dimensions, destination, service level, pickup arrangements, packaging and shipping frequency. For repeat business, share representative parcel sizes and expected monthly volume for a more useful quote.

Transit considerations

Customer-facing delivery estimates depend on destination, selected service, customs processing and shipment details. Confirm available estimates and build a reasonable buffer into order fulfilment — especially for first-time shipments to a new country or for products that may require customs attention.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Final service acceptance, documents and transit estimates depend on the specific shipment and destination.

Can online businesses ship products internationally?

Options depend on the product, destination and available service. Provide the product details, target countries and typical parcel size so the team can confirm suitable options.

Can pickup be arranged from my location?

Pickup availability depends on your location and shipment pattern. Ask the team about pickup options for your business — a single pickup for multiple parcels is often more efficient than individual drop-offs.

Can shipments be tracked?

Tracking availability depends on the selected service. Confirm the tracking method when booking — most international courier services provide online tracking that your customer can also follow.

Can recurring parcels be quoted together?

Yes. Share your expected monthly volume, common destinations and typical parcel dimensions to discuss a business quote. Recurring volume usually produces a more efficient per-parcel cost than one-off pricing.

What items can't be shipped internationally?

Restrictions vary by destination and carrier. Common items requiring extra checks include batteries, liquids, cosmetics, food, aerosols and some consumer electronics. Prohibited goods such as counterfeits, adult items and illegal substances are refused. Confirm before accepting an order.

What documents are needed for e-commerce shipments?

Typically a commercial invoice, packing list, accurate product description and HS code. Some destinations may require additional paperwork depending on the product type. This is a list of possible documents — confirm requirements for your exact shipment.
