UNDERSTAND YOUR LOGISTICS NEEDS

Built around the way your business ships

Garment and textile businesses often need to move buyer samples, fabric swatches, trims, apparel and commercial consignments around production and buyer timelines. The right route depends on the shipment purpose, size, destination and deadline.

JP Express can help you review suitable courier, freight or export-logistics options based on the shipment details you provide. Service selection and requirements should be confirmed for the exact product, route and destination.

DOCUMENTATION & CUSTOMS

Check requirements before dispatch

Depending on the product, transaction and destination, documents may include a commercial invoice, packing list, accurate product description, HS code, certificate of origin or regulatory paperwork. This is a list of possible documents, not a universal checklist.

Product classification, import restrictions, duties and regulatory requirements can vary by destination. Confirm current requirements for your shipment before booking.

PACKAGING & PREPARATION

Prepare your shipment

  • Use clean, dry inner packaging to protect textiles from moisture.
  • Protect samples from crushing, creasing and contamination.
  • Use strong cartons for commercial consignments and secure loose accessories.
  • Label cartons and match shipment details with commercial paperwork.

RESTRICTED ITEMS & COMPLIANCE

Declare contents accurately

Possible considerations

Textiles usually require a clear commercial declaration and product description. Some destinations may apply textile labelling rules, quota or specific import documentation. Always confirm before dispatch.

COST & TRANSIT

Understand what affects your shipment

Shipping cost factors

Cost can vary with actual or volumetric weight, dimensions, shipment quantity, destination, service level, packaging and freight mode. Sample parcels and larger commercial consignments may need different assessments.

Transit considerations

Buyer approvals, production milestones and seasonal deadlines can affect when a shipment should leave. Transit estimates must be confirmed for the selected service and destination.
