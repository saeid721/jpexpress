PACKAGING & PREPARATION

Prepare your shipment

  • Protect components against impact, movement and surface damage.
  • Separate heavy parts from delicate items and use suitable internal support.
  • Declare contents and any batteries, liquids or regulated components accurately.
  • Confirm packaging and acceptance requirements before dispatch.

DOCUMENTATION & CUSTOMS

Check requirements before dispatch

Depending on the product, transaction and destination, documents may include a commercial invoice, packing list, accurate product description, HS code, certificate of origin or regulatory paperwork. This is a list of possible documents, not a universal checklist.

Product classification, import restrictions, duties and regulatory requirements can vary by destination. Confirm current requirements for your shipment before booking.

COST & TRANSIT

Understand what affects your shipment

Shipping cost factors

Pricing can depend on chargeable weight, dimensions, cargo type, quantity, destination, freight mode, packaging and any additional handling needs. Provide a packing list or cargo dimensions where available.

Transit considerations

Production downtime, supplier schedules and project milestones can make timing important. Confirm service availability and transit estimates for the exact origin, destination and cargo details.
