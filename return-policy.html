POLICIES RETURN & REFUND

Clarity on returns & refunds.

This policy outlines how JP Express handles shipment returns, cancellations, refunds, and claims. Please review this carefully before booking your shipment.

Return & Refund Policy

Effective date: 06 October 2026 · This policy is subject to change. Please confirm the latest version with JP Express before relying on it for a specific shipment.

1. Cancellations

You may request a cancellation of your shipment before it has been picked up or processed by our facility. Cancellations requested after pickup may be subject to processing fees and return-to-sender charges.

2. Refunds for Unshipped Items

If you cancel a shipment before it enters our network, you are eligible for a full refund of the shipping fees paid, minus any third-party processing charges. Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

3. Returns & Return Shipping Costs

If a shipment is returned to the sender due to an incorrect address, refusal by the recipient, or customs issues, the customer is responsible for the return shipping costs. This includes the cost of the original outbound shipment and any additional return fees. We strongly recommend confirming recipient details and destination regulations before booking.

4. Lost or Damaged Shipments

Claims for lost or damaged shipments must be reported to JP Express immediately. All claims must be submitted with the original receipt, photos of the damaged goods, and packaging. The maximum liability for lost or damaged shipments is limited to the declared value or the shipping cost, whichever is lower, unless additional insurance was purchased. We are not liable for damages caused by poor packaging.

5. Customs & Duties

JP Express is not responsible for refunds related to customs delays, confiscated items, or unpaid duties and taxes. These are the sole responsibility of the customer. Any shipment refused by customs will be considered abandoned and no refund will be issued.

6. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days. The time may vary depending on your bank or payment provider. Please note that we cannot refund any third-party service fees (e.g., payment gateway charges) incurred at the time of purchase.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team with your tracking number and the reason for the request. Our team will review your case and respond within 2 business days.
