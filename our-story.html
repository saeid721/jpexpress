OUR STORY

Built in Bangladesh, Global by Design

We exist to make international shipping simple, reliable, and transparent for people and businesses in Bangladesh.

Who We Are

A logistics partner built for modern global trade.

JP Express is a Bangladesh-based international courier and logistics company. We help individuals, families, online sellers, and exporters send documents, parcels, samples, and commercial cargo across the world. Our goal is to remove the friction from international shipping by providing clear information, reliable service, and accessible support.

From our base in Dhaka, we coordinate the entire journey—from pickup and documentation to customs support and final delivery—so our customers can focus on what matters most to them.

Our Journey

Milestones in motion.

Beginning

Started with international courier.

We began by focusing on what customers needed most: a reliable way to send documents and parcels abroad from Bangladesh.

Growth

Expanded into freight and export.

As our customers' needs grew, we added air freight, sea freight, and export logistics services to support larger and more complex shipments.

Today

One partner for the entire journey.

Today, JP Express offers a complete suite of logistics solutions, from documentation and customs support to shipment tracking, all under one roof.

Our Direction

What guides us.

Reliability

How it works

A simple, connected journey.

  1. Share Your Requirement

    Tell us what you need to ship, where it's going, and your timeline. We'll help you understand the available options.

  2. Confirm Service & Quote

    We provide a clear quote based on your shipment details. Once you confirm, we'll guide you through the required documents.

  3. Arrange Pickup or Drop-off

    Schedule a convenient pickup from your location or drop off your shipment at our facility. We'll handle the rest.

  4. Track & Receive Updates

    Follow your shipment's journey with our tracking tools and receive updates from dispatch to delivery.

Our Promise

Clarity and support, at every step.

We believe that reliable shipping starts with clear communication. Our commitment is to provide you with accurate information about your shipment's journey, from the moment it's booked until it's safely delivered.

  • Practical service principles focused on clear communication.
  • Shipment visibility and status updates you can depend on.
  • Accessible support to answer your questions and help you plan.
