Our Story

Built on Reliability, Grown by Trust

JP Express began as a small courier desk in Gulshan, Dhaka, with a simple promise: deliver every shipment on time, every time. That promise has taken us from a single city route to a logistics network connecting Bangladeshi exporters, e-commerce brands and individuals to over 220 countries worldwide.

  1. 2014Founded in Dhaka

    Started as a domestic courier service for local businesses.

  2. 2017Launched International Courier

    Expanded into express document & parcel delivery worldwide.

  3. 2020Air & Sea Freight Added

    Introduced full freight and export logistics solutions.

  4. Today220+ Countries, 50K+ Shipments

    A trusted logistics partner for businesses across Bangladesh.

Purpose

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To simplify logistics for every business and individual in Bangladesh by delivering fast, reliable and transparent courier, freight and customs solutions — backed by real-time tracking and dedicated support at every step.

Our Vision

To become Bangladesh's most trusted logistics partner, recognized worldwide for connecting local businesses to global markets with the same care, speed and integrity, shipment after shipment.
