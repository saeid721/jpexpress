Company Overview

A Bangladesh-Based Logistics Partner for a Global World

JP Express is an international courier and export logistics company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We make global shipping simple for people and businesses that need to send documents, personal parcels, product samples, e-commerce orders and commercial cargo overseas.

From booking and pickup to documentation, customs support and shipment tracking, one dedicated team keeps your shipment moving and keeps you informed.

Our Story

Built Around What Our Customers Need to Ship

JP Express started with international courier for documents and parcels. As customers asked for more, our services grew to cover air freight, sea freight, export logistics and customs support, so businesses in Bangladesh can reach global markets through a single partner.

Today we continue to expand our destinations and services around one principle: reliable shipping, honest communication and support you can reach when it matters.
