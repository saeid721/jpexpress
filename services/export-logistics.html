Service overview

What is Export Logistics?

Export logistics is the managed process of moving goods from Bangladesh to an overseas buyer. It covers export documentation, freight booking, customs coordination, consolidation and delivery, tied together in one workflow.

Quick answer

Does JP Express provide export logistics from Bangladesh?

Yes. JP Express supports exporters with export documentation, air and sea freight coordination, customs guidance, pickup, consolidation and end-to-end tracking. Small parcels and documents usually move via International Courier, and time-sensitive cargo via Air Freight.

How the service works

A clear path from export enquiry to delivery

The customer journey stays simple, while operational details are handled by the right team. The number of steps reflects the real export workflow.

Transit time

Estimated — not guaranteed

Transit times are estimates. The real delivery window depends on the mode, route, documentation, customs and final delivery.

Freight mode & route

Air, sea and courier have very different transit windows. Direct and connecting routes also differ.

Documentation quality

Accurate, complete export documents reduce the risk of delays at origin and destination customs.

Customs processing

Export and destination customs may need additional information, which can extend the timeline.

Pickup, delivery & holidays

Pickup time, delivery location, weekends, public holidays and peak periods can all shift the delivery date.

Any transit figure is an estimate. JP Express does not promise a guaranteed delivery date unless a guaranteed service has been explicitly confirmed.

Pricing

What affects the cost

The final price is confirmed in your quote. The factors below explain why two export shipments can cost differently.

Origin & destination

Origin city, port or airport, destination country and final delivery location affect the rate and the transit.

Weight & volume

Chargeable weight (actual or volumetric, depending on mode) and total volume drive the base freight cost.

Dimensions & packaging

Bulky or irregular cargo can cost more. Packaging and palletization are usually quoted separately.

Service scope

Documentation only, freight coordination, customs guidance, consolidation and pickup are each scoped and priced differently.

Documentation complexity

Multiple certificates, licensed products or unusual destination requirements can add to the paperwork effort.

Customs, duties & taxes

Export and destination customs charges and import duties depend on the goods and country, and are usually separate from freight.

Additional services

Pickup, insurance, warehousing, consolidation or destination brokerage can add to the price.

Surcharges

Fuel, security and other applicable surcharges may be applied to the base rate.

Customs & compliance

Customs processing is destination-led

Customs rules are set by the destination country and can vary by item, value, shipment type and sender/receiver status. JP Express does not present customs information as universal.

What customs may require

Export declaration at origin, destination import clearance, duties and taxes, accurate documentation, product restrictions and country-specific regulations.

Where to check further

Restricted & prohibited items

Know the three categories

Destination and carrier rules change, so this list is a starting point — not a universal policy.

Prohibited items

Items JP Express cannot export. Examples include illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods and undeclared dangerous goods.

Restricted items

Items that may need conditions, documents or approval — such as batteries, chemicals, medicines and high-value goods.

Destination-specific

Eligibility can change by destination. A legally accepted item in one country may be restricted in another.

Categories to check before you export

Packaging & handling

Pack for safe export transit

Good packaging protects the cargo and helps avoid avoidable delays. Fragile or special-handling cargo should be flagged at booking.

Packaging expectations

  • Use strong, correctly sized export cartons
  • Cushion fragile items on all sides
  • Use export-grade pallets for heavy cargo
  • Show shipping marks and full buyer address
  • Mark fragile items where needed
  • Show weight and dimensions accurately on the cargo

Extra handling

Fragile, high-value or unusually shaped cargo may benefit from extra protection or cargo insurance. Label dimensions and weight accurately so we can confirm the right handling.
