Service overview

What is Air Freight?

Air freight moves commercial cargo by aircraft from Bangladesh to another country. JP Express offers it as Airport-to-Airport, Door-to-Door and Express Air Cargo service for time-sensitive shipments.

Quick answer

Does JP Express provide air freight from Bangladesh?

Yes. JP Express, based in Dhaka, moves commercial cargo by air from Bangladesh to supported destinations with Airport-to-Airport, Door-to-Door and Express Air Cargo options. Small individual parcels are usually better sent by International Courier, while large non-urgent volumes fit Sea Freight.

Transit time

Estimated — not guaranteed

Transit times are estimates. The real delivery window depends on the route, airline capacity, cargo characteristics, customs and final delivery.

Route & airline capacity

Direct and connecting routes differ in transit, and airline space availability can affect when cargo departs.

Cargo characteristics

Weight, dimensions, contents and the accuracy of declared information can affect handling time.

Customs processing

Destination customs may need additional information, which can extend the timeline independent of the airline.

Pickup, delivery & holidays

Pickup time, delivery location, weekends, public holidays and peak periods can all shift the delivery date.

Any transit figure is an estimate. JP Express does not promise a guaranteed delivery date unless a guaranteed service has been explicitly confirmed.

Pricing

What affects the cost

The final price is confirmed in your quote. The factors below explain why two cargo shipments of the same weight can cost differently.

Route & destination

Origin airport, destination airport, country and sometimes final city affect the rate and the transit.

Chargeable weight

Air freight is typically charged on the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight, according to airline rules.

Dimensions & quantity

Bulky cargo can cost more than its weight suggests. Number of pieces and pallet counts can also affect the price.

Service level

Airport-to-airport, door-to-door and express air cargo are priced differently because their handling and speed differ.

Packaging, palletization & handling

Special packaging, palletization or fragile handling are usually charged separately.

Customs, duties & taxes

Duties and import taxes depend on the destination, product and value, and are usually separate from freight charges.

Additional services

Insurance, pickup, warehousing, customs assistance or other optional services can add to the price.

Surcharges

Fuel, security and other applicable surcharges may be applied to the base rate.

Customs & compliance

Customs processing is destination-led

Customs rules are set by the destination country and can vary by item, value, shipment type and sender/receiver status. JP Express does not present customs information as universal.

What customs may require

Customs processing, duties and taxes, accurate documentation, product restrictions and destination regulations. The exact requirement depends on the goods and the country.

Restricted & prohibited items

Know the three categories

Airline and destination rules change, so this list is a starting point — not a universal policy.

Prohibited items

Items JP Express cannot accept by air. Examples include illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods and undeclared dangerous goods.

Restricted items

Items that may need conditions, documents or approval — such as batteries, liquids, chemicals and high-value goods.

Destination-specific

Eligibility can change by destination. A legally accepted item in one country may be restricted in another.

Categories to check before you ship

Packaging & handling

Pack for safe air transit

Good packaging protects the cargo and helps avoid avoidable delays. Fragile or special-handling cargo should be flagged at booking.

Packaging expectations

  • Use strong, correctly sized outer cartons
  • Cushion fragile items on all sides
  • Use pallets for heavy or stacked cargo
  • Write the full recipient address clearly
  • Mark fragile items where needed
  • Show weight and dimensions accurately on the cargo

Extra handling

Fragile, high-value or unusually shaped cargo may benefit from extra protection or cargo insurance. Label dimensions and weight accurately so we can confirm the right handling.

Frequently asked questions

Air freight questions

These answers are general. Confirm current availability and shipment-specific requirements with JP Express.

Air freight is the movement of commercial cargo by aircraft. It is usually chosen for time-sensitive shipments and goods where faster transit matters more than the lowest cost.

Air freight is usually preferred when speed matters, volumes are small to medium, or the goods are high-value or time-critical. Sea freight is generally more economical for larger, non-urgent cargo.

General commercial cargo, samples, electronics, machinery parts, apparel and similar goods can be shipped by air when permitted by the destination and carrier. Dangerous goods and certain restricted items require special handling or are not accepted.

Air freight is typically priced by chargeable weight, which is the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight, plus applicable surcharges and destination charges. Request a quote for a shipment-specific price.

Transit time is an estimate. It depends on the route, airline capacity, service, customs processing and final delivery, and is not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed.

Yes. You receive a reference or tracking number at booking and can check the shipment status through the JP Express tracking page.

Commercial shipments usually need a commercial invoice, packing list and export documentation. Destination-specific certificates may also be required.

Door-to-door air freight is available on supported routes and depends on pickup and delivery locations. Confirm with JP Express when requesting a quote.
