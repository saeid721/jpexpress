Service overview

What is International Courier?

International courier moves documents and parcels from Bangladesh to another country, with pickup, tracking and delivery handled by one provider. JP Express offers it as Express, Economy and Door-to-Door service.

Quick answer

Does JP Express provide international courier from Bangladesh?

Yes. JP Express, based in Dhaka, sends documents and parcels from Bangladesh to supported destinations with Express, Economy and Door-to-Door options. Larger commercial volumes are usually better handled by Air Freight or Sea Freight.

Transit time

Estimated — not guaranteed

Transit times are estimates. The real delivery window depends on the destination, service, shipment characteristics, customs and last-mile delivery.

Destination & service

Express and Economy have different estimated transit windows. Remote destinations can take longer than major cities on the same route.

Shipment characteristics

Weight, dimensions, contents and the accuracy of declared information can affect handling time.

Customs processing

Destination customs may need additional information, which can extend the timeline independent of the carrier.

Pickup, delivery & holidays

Pickup time, delivery location, weekends, public holidays and peak periods can all shift the delivery date.

Any transit figure is an estimate. JP Express does not promise a guaranteed delivery date unless a guaranteed service has been explicitly confirmed.

Pricing

What affects the cost

The final price is confirmed in your quote. The factors below explain why two parcels of the same weight can cost differently.

Origin & destination

Route, country and sometimes city or postal code affect the rate and the transit.

Actual & volumetric weight

The chargeable weight is usually the higher of actual and volumetric weight, according to the applicable rules.

Dimensions & quantity

Bulky parcels can cost more than their weight suggests. Number of pieces can also affect the price.

Service level

Express, Economy and Door-to-Door are priced differently because their handling and speed differ.

Packaging & special handling

Special packaging, fragile handling or extra services are usually charged separately.

Customs, duties & taxes

Duties and import taxes depend on the destination, product and value, and are usually separate from shipping charges.

Additional services

Insurance, pickup, packaging or other optional services can add to the price.

Surcharges

Fuel and other applicable surcharges may be applied to the base rate.

Customs & compliance

Customs processing is destination-led

Customs rules are set by the destination country and can vary by item, value, shipment type and sender/receiver status. JP Express does not present customs information as universal.

What customs may require

Customs processing, duties and taxes, accurate documentation, product restrictions and destination regulations. The exact requirement depends on the goods and the country.

Restricted & prohibited items

Know the three categories

Destination and carrier rules change, so this list is a starting point — not a universal policy.

Prohibited items

Items JP Express cannot accept. Examples include illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods and certain hazardous materials.

Restricted items

Items that may need conditions, documents or approval — such as batteries, liquids, medicines, food and high-value goods.

Destination-specific

Eligibility can change by destination. A legally accepted item in one country may be restricted in another.

Packaging & handling

Pack for safe international transit

Good packaging protects the item and helps avoid avoidable delivery issues. Fragile or special-handling items should be flagged at booking.

Packaging expectations

  • Use a strong, correctly sized outer box
  • Cushion fragile items on all sides
  • Seal liquids in leak-proof bags
  • Write the full recipient address clearly
  • Mark fragile items where needed
  • Show weight and dimensions accurately on the parcel

Extra handling

Fragile, high-value or unusually shaped items may benefit from extra protection or cargo insurance. Label dimensions and weight accurately so we can confirm the right handling.
