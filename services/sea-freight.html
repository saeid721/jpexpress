Service overview

What is Sea Freight?

Sea freight moves cargo by ship from Bangladesh to another country. JP Express offers it as FCL, LCL and Door-to-Door service for bulk and heavy cargo where cost per unit matters more than speed.

Quick answer

Does JP Express provide sea freight from Bangladesh?

Yes. JP Express, based in Dhaka, moves bulk cargo by sea from Bangladesh to supported destinations with FCL, LCL and Door-to-Door options. Urgent or smaller cargo is usually better handled by Air Freight, and small parcels by International Courier.

Transit time

Estimated — not guaranteed

Transit times are estimates. The real delivery window depends on the route, vessel schedules, cargo characteristics, customs and final delivery.

Route & vessel schedule

Direct and transhipment routes differ in transit, and vessel sailing dates can shift the arrival window.

Cargo characteristics

Volume, weight, container type and the accuracy of declared information can affect handling time.

Customs processing

Destination customs may need additional information, which can extend the timeline independent of the shipping line.

Pickup, delivery & holidays

Pickup time, delivery location, port congestion, weekends and public holidays can all shift the delivery date.

Any transit figure is an estimate. JP Express does not promise a guaranteed delivery date unless a guaranteed service has been explicitly confirmed.

Pricing

What affects the cost

The final price is confirmed in your quote. The factors below explain why two sea cargo shipments of the same weight can cost differently.

Origin port & destination port

Port pair, country and sometimes final city affect the rate and the transit.

Container size or LCL volume

FCL is priced per container, while LCL is priced by cubic meter or weight, according to the shipping line rules.

Weight & dimensions

Heavy or bulky cargo can cost more. Total volume and number of pieces also affect the price.

Service level

FCL, LCL and door-to-door are priced differently because their handling and speed differ.

Packaging, palletization & handling

Special packaging, palletization or fragile handling are usually charged separately.

Customs, duties & taxes

Duties and import taxes depend on the destination, product and value, and are usually separate from freight charges.

Additional services

Insurance, pickup, warehousing, consolidation or customs assistance can add to the price.

Surcharges

Fuel, security, port and other applicable surcharges may be applied to the base rate.

Customs processing is destination-led

Customs rules are set by the destination country and can vary by item, value, shipment type and sender/receiver status. JP Express does not present customs information as universal.

Customs processing, duties and taxes, accurate documentation, product restrictions and destination regulations. The exact requirement depends on the goods and the country.

Restricted & prohibited items

Know the three categories

Shipping line and destination rules change, so this list is a starting point — not a universal policy.

Prohibited items

Items JP Express cannot ship by sea. Examples include illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods and undeclared dangerous goods.

Restricted items

Items that may need conditions, documents or approval — such as batteries, liquids, chemicals and high-value goods.

Destination-specific

Eligibility can change by destination. A legally accepted item in one country may be restricted in another.

Packaging & handling

Pack for safe ocean transit

Good packaging protects the cargo and helps avoid avoidable delays. Fragile or special-handling cargo should be flagged at booking.

Packaging expectations

  • Use strong, correctly sized export cartons
  • Use export-grade pallets for heavy cargo
  • Secure cargo for container stuffing
  • Show shipping marks and full recipient address
  • Mark fragile items where needed
  • Show weight and dimensions accurately on the cargo

Extra handling

Fragile, high-value or unusually shaped cargo may benefit from extra protection or cargo insurance. Label dimensions and weight accurately so we can confirm the right handling.

Frequently asked questions

Sea freight questions

These answers are general. Confirm current availability and shipment-specific requirements with JP Express.

Sea freight is the movement of cargo by ship. It is usually chosen for larger, heavier or non-urgent shipments because it is typically more economical per unit than air freight.

FCL (Full Container Load) means the whole container is used for one shipment. LCL (Less than Container Load) means cargo shares a container with other shipments, which suits smaller volumes.

Sea freight is usually preferred when volume is large, cargo is heavy or non-urgent, and cost per unit matters more than speed. Air freight is better for urgent or smaller shipments.

General commercial cargo, apparel, machinery, raw materials and bulk goods can be shipped by sea when permitted by the destination and carrier. Dangerous goods and restricted items require special handling or are not accepted.

Sea freight is usually priced by container (FCL) or by cubic meter or weight (LCL), plus origin and destination charges, surcharges and any additional services. Request a quote for a shipment-specific price.

Transit time is an estimate. It depends on the route, port schedules, service, customs processing and final delivery, and is not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed.

Yes. You receive a reference or tracking number at booking and can check the shipment status through the JP Express tracking page.

Commercial shipments usually need a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and export documentation. Destination-specific certificates may also be required.

Duties and import taxes depend on the destination, product and declared value, and are usually separate from freight charges. Confirm current rules before booking.

Door-to-door sea freight is available on supported routes and depends on pickup and delivery locations. Confirm with JP Express when requesting a quote.

Service availability, routes, prices, transit estimates, document requirements and cargo acceptance are confirmed per shipment. Do not dispatch restricted or uncertain cargo before receiving guidance.
