Service overview

What are Import Solutions?

Import solutions cover the services needed to bring goods into Bangladesh from an overseas supplier. It combines sourcing coordination, freight booking, customs guidance, documentation and delivery in one workflow.

Quick answer

Does JP Express provide import solutions into Bangladesh?

Yes. JP Express supports importers with overseas sourcing coordination, import freight by air or sea, customs clearance guidance, import documentation and delivery within Bangladesh. Small parcels and documents usually move via International Courier, and time-sensitive cargo via Air Freight.

Transit time

Estimated — not guaranteed

Transit times are estimates. The real delivery window depends on the origin, mode, documentation, customs and final delivery.

Origin & freight mode

Air, sea and courier have very different transit windows. Direct and connecting routes also differ.

Documentation quality

Accurate, complete import documents reduce the risk of delays at origin and Bangladesh customs.

Customs processing

Origin and Bangladesh customs may need additional information, which can extend the timeline.

Pickup, delivery & holidays

Pickup time, delivery location, weekends, public holidays and peak periods can all shift the delivery date.

Any transit figure is an estimate. JP Express does not promise a guaranteed delivery date unless a guaranteed service has been explicitly confirmed.

Pricing

What affects the cost

The final price is confirmed in your quote. The factors below explain why two import shipments can cost differently.

Origin & destination

Origin country, port or airport and final delivery location in Bangladesh affect the rate and the transit.

Weight & volume

Chargeable weight (actual or volumetric, depending on mode) and total volume drive the base freight cost.

Dimensions & packaging

Bulky or irregular cargo can cost more. Packaging and palletization are usually quoted separately.

Service scope

Sourcing coordination, freight booking, customs guidance, documentation and delivery are each scoped and priced differently.

Documentation complexity

Multiple certificates, regulated products or unusual origin requirements can add to the paperwork effort.

Customs, duties & taxes

Bangladesh import duties, VAT and other taxes depend on the product and origin, and are usually separate from freight.

Additional services

Pickup, insurance, warehousing, consolidation or clearing agent coordination can add to the price.

Surcharges

Fuel, security and other applicable surcharges may be applied to the base rate.

Customs & compliance

Customs processing is destination-led

Bangladesh import rules are set by Bangladesh Customs and can vary by item, value, shipment type and importer status. JP Express does not present customs information as universal.

What customs may require

Bangladesh import clearance, duties, VAT and taxes, accurate documentation, product restrictions and country-specific regulations.

Restricted & prohibited items

Know the three categories

Origin and destination rules change, so this list is a starting point — not a universal policy.

Prohibited items

Items JP Express cannot import. Examples include illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods and undeclared dangerous goods.

Restricted items

Items that may need conditions, documents or approval — such as batteries, chemicals, medicines and high-value goods.

Destination-specific

Eligibility can change by origin and Bangladesh rules. A legally accepted item in one country may be restricted on import.

Packaging & handling

Pack for safe import transit

Good packaging protects the cargo and helps avoid avoidable delays. Fragile or special-handling cargo should be flagged at booking.

Packaging expectations

  • Use strong, correctly sized export cartons
  • Cushion fragile items on all sides
  • Use export-grade pallets for heavy cargo
  • Show shipping marks and full importer address
  • Mark fragile items where needed
  • Show weight and dimensions accurately on the cargo

Extra handling

Fragile, high-value or unusually shaped cargo may benefit from extra protection or cargo insurance. Label dimensions and weight accurately so we can confirm the right handling.
