Packaging Guide

Export-grade packing techniques that protect your goods through long-distance transit.

Do

  • Use double-walled boxes for anything over 5 kg.
  • Wrap fragile items individually with bubble wrap.
  • Fill empty space so nothing shifts in transit.
  • Label the box "Fragile" if it contains breakables.
  • Keep a copy of the invoice inside and outside the box.

Don't

  • Reuse damaged or crushed boxes for valuable items.
  • Leave liquids or powders loose without sealed bags.
  • Overfill boxes so the lid bulges or won't close flat.
  • Forget to remove old shipping labels and barcodes.
  • Use string, cloth wrap or tape-only closures.

Restricted & Prohibited Items

Some items can't be shipped, or need special handling and declarations. Common categories below.

Lithium Batteries

Loose or damaged batteries are prohibited; devices with built-in batteries need declaration.

Liquids & Aerosols

Flammable liquids, pressurized aerosols and perfumes over set limits are restricted.

Weapons & Ammunition

Firearms, ammunition and weapon replicas cannot be shipped through our network.

Perishable Food

Fresh food and certain agricultural products are restricted or need import permits.

Currency & Valuables

Cash, bearer bonds and precious metals require special declared-value handling.

Counterfeit Goods

Replica or counterfeit branded goods are prohibited under international trade law.

Pharmaceuticals

Prescription medicines often need import licenses and cannot be shipped without one.

Plant & Organic Material

Seeds, soil and plant products are restricted in many countries for biosecurity reasons.

Not sure about an item?

Check with our team before booking — restrictions vary by destination country.
