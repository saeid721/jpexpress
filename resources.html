Export documentation checklistExport

Most export shipments need a commercial invoice and a packing list. Depending on the goods and destination you may also need a certificate of origin, permits or other certificates.

HS codes explainedExport

An HS (Harmonized System) code classifies your goods. Customs use it to apply duties, taxes and rules. Use the correct code on your invoice, and ask us if you are unsure which one applies.

Volumetric weightCost & Transit

Bulky, light parcels are priced by size as well as weight. Volumetric weight is length × width × height (cm) divided by a divisor. The higher of actual and volumetric weight is usually the chargeable weight. The divisor varies by service.

Packaging Dos and Don'ts

Good packing protects your goods and reduces avoidable delays.

Do

  • Use a strong, undamaged box that suits the weight
  • Wrap fragile items individually
  • Fill empty space so nothing shifts in transit
  • Seal seams with strong tape
  • Put full recipient details on the box and keep a copy of your invoice

Don't

  • Reuse crushed or damaged boxes for valuable items
  • Leave liquids or powders loose without sealing them
  • Overfill the box so the lid bulges
  • Leave old labels or barcodes on the box
  • Use string or cloth instead of proper tape

Restricted & Prohibited Items

Some items cannot be shipped, or need special handling. These are common categories.

  • Batteries

    Lithium and loose batteries are often restricted or need special handling.

  • Liquids and aerosols

    Flammable liquids, aerosols and some cosmetics may be restricted.

  • Weapons and ammunition

    Weapons, ammunition and replicas are not accepted.

  • Food and perishables

    Many destinations restrict food and agricultural products.

  • Cash and valuables

    Cash and high-value items need special handling or may not be accepted.

  • Counterfeit goods

    Counterfeit and imitation goods are prohibited.

  • Medicines

    Medicines are often controlled and may need approval.

  • Plants and seeds

    Plant material is restricted in many countries.

Restrictions vary by destination and carrier and can change. Please confirm with our team before booking. We are not a customs authority.
