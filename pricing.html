Fuel Surcharge

How Fuel Surcharge Works

Fuel surcharge is calculated as a percentage of your base freight cost and is applied automatically at checkout. Rates are reviewed monthly based on regional fuel price movements and may change without prior notice.

Surcharge = Base Freight Cost × Surcharge %

Cost is based on chargeable weight (the higher of actual and volumetric weight), destination, service type and the current fuel surcharge.

Volumetric weight is calculated from a package's dimensions (length × width × height ÷ a service-specific divisor) and applies when a package is large but light.

Fuel surcharge is a variable percentage added to freight cost to account for fluctuating fuel prices. It differs by service type and is reviewed monthly.
