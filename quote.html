Weight

Actual vs Volumetric Weight

Light but bulky parcels take up a lot of space. For pricing, carriers compare the actual weight with the volumetric weight and use the higher one as the chargeable weight.

The divisor can differ by service, so the calculator uses the applicable rule and our team confirms the final chargeable weight in your quote.

Volumetric weight

Length × Width × Height (cm) ÷ divisor

Example (illustrative): a 40 × 30 × 30 cm box is 36,000 cm³. With a divisor of 5,000, volumetric weight is 7.2 kg. If it actually weighs 3 kg, the chargeable weight is 7.2 kg.

Pricing Factors

What Affects Your Shipping Cost

Shipping cost depends on these details, so two parcels of the same weight can price differently.

  • Destination

    Country, and sometimes city or postal code, affects the rate and transit.

  • Service Level

    Express, economy, air or sea freight are priced differently.

  • Chargeable Weight

    The billable weight is based on actual or volumetric weight.

  • Dimensions

    Bulky packages can cost more than their weight suggests.

  • Shipment Type

    Documents, parcels, samples and commercial cargo differ.

  • Packaging

    Special packing or handling may add to the cost.

  • Duties & Taxes

    Customs charges depend on the destination and goods.

  • Additional Charges

    Surcharges, pickup or extra services may apply.
